Keeping the numbers honest

Anyone could send made-up numbers, so nothing is taken on trust.

15 minutes non-stop

Each run is 15 minutes of non-stop farming on one map with the same party. Breaks don't count.

One player, one vote

Each player counts once (their typical run), however much they play.

Outliers left out

Runs under half or over double what others get on that map are dropped.

3 players or more

A map is only ranked once 3 different players have farmed it. Before that, it's early data: hidden at first, and greyed out when you show it.

Questions

What gets shared?

Only 15-minute runs of non-stop farming, and only if you said yes in the overlay: the map, when you farmed it, your level, job and party size, the EXP and mesos you got, and a random ID for your copy of the overlay. Never your character's name. You can stop sharing any time in the overlay's settings. The newest runs also show in the bar at the very top (level, job, map, party and EXP/hr, rounded), with nothing about who shared them.

Why is Path greyed out?

Under Lv 30 you're still 1st job, and everyone plays the same until they pick their 2nd job, so you see what every player of your class gets around your level. Path opens at Lv 30. From then on, each path only counts its own jobs: Shadower is Bandits, Chief Bandits and Shadowers, for example.

Is the overlay safe?

It only looks at your screen, like a screen recorder. It never touches the game's files or memory and never presses keys or clicks for you. It's open source, so anyone can check. It isn't made by or affiliated with Nexon, so like any third-party tool, you use it at your own risk.

Does it cost anything?

No. The overlay and this site are free, made by a player for the community.

How do I read the chart?

Each row is a map, and every row uses the same scale, so further right means more EXP/hr. Each dot is one player: their typical run on that map, however much they've played it. The gold line is the typical EXP/hr there (half of players get more, half less), and the shaded bar is what the middle half of players get. A short bar means most players get about the same; a long one means gear, skills and how busy the map is make a big difference. Hollow red rings are runs left out of the numbers for being under half or over double what others get, like an AFK player or a leech. Point at anything (or tap it) for the exact numbers.

Why isn't my map or class here yet?

A map is ranked once 3 different players around your level (close to your level: a map starts with players within 3 levels of yours and only goes up to 5 if it needs more players, and says so), on the same path, and solo or in a party like you, have shared a run there. Until then, it's early data: untick Hide early data to see what's come in so far, greyed out. A new player's runs start counting a day after their first one, and once they have a few, so a flood of made-up players can't change the numbers at once. Map names that the game doesn't have (an event map, say) show once 3 players have shared them on 2 different days. The more people share, the faster it fills in. You can help by sharing yours!
